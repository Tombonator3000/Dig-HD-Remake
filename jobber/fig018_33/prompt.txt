Task: faithful HD remaster of a sheet of animation frames from the 1995 adventure game The Dig (LucasArts). This is a production asset. The game engine cuts every frame out of the sheet by its exact position and outline and draws it in place of the original sprite, so the geometry must not change at all.

Input: the attached image is the original low-resolution sheet enlarged 4x with hard pixel edges. The frames stand apart on a plain flat cyan background (#00C8C8). Around the sheet is a plain gray (#808080) border. The border is only padding.

All frames on this sheet are animation frames of the same character (Maggie Robbins), in different poses. On this sheet, row by row from left to right: animation 49, facing right (4 frames); animation 50, facing right (1 frame); animation 51, facing right (5 frames).

Do:
- Repaint every frame in high resolution with clean, natural detail: smooth shading instead of dithering, crisp outlines, believable cloth, skin, hair and material.
- Keep the original look: the same painted 1990s LucasArts style, the same colors, light direction, shadows and contrast.
- Make all frames look identical apart from the pose: the same face, hair, clothes, colors and light in every frame. The frames are played one after another in the game, so any difference between them flickers.
- Keep the outline of every frame exactly as in the input: the same size, pose and position, pixel for pixel.

Do not:
- Do not move, zoom, crop, rotate, mirror or resize any frame, and do not change the spacing between the frames.
- Do not add, remove, merge or redesign frames. Do not add anything between the frames: no shadows, floor, text, numbers, labels, frames or grid lines.
- Some frames show only part of the character, for example a body without a head or a head alone, because the game draws the parts separately. Paint them as they are and do not complete them.
- Keep the background plain and flat in exactly the same color (#00C8C8) everywhere around the frames, with no gradient or texture. Keep the gray border plain gray (#808080).
- Small bright magenta marks under the feet or at the edges are shadow markers used by the game. Keep them as they are.
- No photorealism, no modern 3D look, no film grain, no vignette, no watermark, no signature.

Output: one image, exactly 1536 x 1024 pixels, with the same layout as the input.

If a second image is attached, it is an already approved HD sheet of the same character, or an approved HD picture from the same game. Paint the character exactly as in that image: the same face, hair, clothes, colors and level of detail. Take all poses, outlines and positions from the first image.
